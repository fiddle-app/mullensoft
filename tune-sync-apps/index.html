Tune-Sync Apps

These apps are all clients of the same sync folder — point them at one shared folder in your cloud drive of choice (for example iCloud Drive/TuneSync) and your tunes, recordings, and practice data flow between them automatically.

The apps

  • Tune Hub

    Your tune library's home base, on desktop. Curate tunes, versions, and recordings, track sources and performers, and manage your practice backlog.

  • Timeliner

    Annotate and review your lesson recordings — mark up video or audio from lessons and practice sessions and link the markers back to the tunes you're working on.

  • Jam Buddy

    Your jam companion — pull up tune lists for a session, track who you played with, and work through a practice backlog playlist between tunes.

  • Practice Buddy

    A practice timer with built-in micro-breaks that also carries your daily practice queue, so what you're due to work on follows you to the timer.

One sync folder, several apps

Each app is a client of the sync folder you choose — nothing is stored on our servers. Add or remove apps from the same folder at any time; they'll pick up each other's changes without any extra setup.
