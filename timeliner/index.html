Timeliner

Timeliner helps you review your fiddle lessons and practice recordings. Load a video or audio recording, split it into time segments, and attach notes to each segment, so you can jump straight to the part you want to work on.

What it does

  • Projects and Timelines

    The ＋ button starts a project from your Photo Library, the Files app or your Apple Music Library; open it and add segments and notes along its timeline.

  • Transcript

    The optional transcript turns speech in a recording into a searchable timeline, entirely on your device.

  • Loop, speed and pitch

    Loop a passage and change its speed and pitch to work out a tune.

  • No account

    Your recordings and notes stay on your device and, if you choose, in a shared library in your own iCloud Drive.
