Timeliner — Support

Timeliner helps you review your fiddle lessons and practice recordings. Load a video or audio recording, split it into time segments, and attach notes to each segment — so you can jump straight to the part you want to work on.

Tips & FAQ

  • Your recordings and notes stay on your device and in your own iCloud — there's no account and no sign-in.
  • Add a recording from the Files app or your Photos library, then define segments and annotate them along the timeline.
  • The optional transcript feature turns speech in a recording into a searchable timeline, and runs entirely on your device — nothing is uploaded.
  • If a recording shows as “in iCloud,” it just hasn't downloaded to this device yet — tap to download it, and it becomes playable.
  • Using more than one device? Timeliner keeps your projects in sync through your own iCloud.
