Timeliner — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 17, 2026

Timeliner (“the app”), developed by Casey Mullen (MullenSoft), is designed so that it collects, stores, and transmits no personal data. Your recordings and everything you create stay on your device and in your own iCloud.

Data collection

Timeliner does not collect any personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. Your recordings, timelines, annotations, and tune library are stored locally on your device — and, if you use sync, in your own iCloud — and are never sent to the developer or any server we operate.

Your media and iCloud sync

The recordings you annotate remain your files. When you add a recording, the app references or copies it into your own storage. If you enable syncing, Timeliner moves your data only between your own devices through your personal iCloud account — the developer has no access to it and operates no server in between. Your use of iCloud is governed by Apple's terms.

Transcription (on device)

The optional transcript feature converts speech in your recordings to text using Apple's speech recognition with on-device recognition required. Your audio is not sent to Apple's servers, to the developer, or to anyone else for transcription — it is processed entirely on your device.

Microphone

Timeliner does not record from your microphone. It only imports and plays back existing recordings; it never captures live audio.

Network usage

The app does not send any information about you over the network. It works with files on your device and in your own iCloud. It contains no analytics or tracking that would transmit usage data.

Third-party services

Timeliner uses no third-party services beyond Apple's iCloud (for your own device-to-device sync, described above) and — for this policy page — hosting on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. GitHub's standard server logs may record your IP address and the URL you requested when you visit this page. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Children's privacy

Timeliner is appropriate for users of all ages. Because it collects no personal data and transmits nothing to us, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by default.

Changes

If we make any material change to this policy, we will post the new version at this URL and update the effective date at the top.
