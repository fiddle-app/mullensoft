Jam Buddy — Support

Jam Buddy keeps your jams, the tunes you play and the recordings you make. See which jams are coming up, filter the tune list to the jam's key and tuning, check tunes off as they are played, and record a take without leaving the list.

Tips & FAQ

  • There is no account and no sign-in. Jam Buddy never needs iCloud.
  • On first launch, choose Start without a shared library, then Start with a pre-populated library if you want the tune list filled with common old-time tunes.
  • To share tunes and recordings with Timeliner and the other tune-sync apps, connect a shared library in a folder in your own cloud drive. See compatible apps and cloud sync tips.
  • Recording asks for the Microphone the first time. Location, Photos and Apple Music are asked for only when you use a feature that needs them; change any of them in Settings ▸ Jam Buddy.
  • A typed address, a pasted YouTube link and a pasted Slippery Hill link are sent to OpenStreetMap, YouTube and Slippery Hill only to fill in a place or a title. See the privacy policy.
