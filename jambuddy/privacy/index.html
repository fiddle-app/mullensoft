Jam Buddy — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

Jam Buddy (“the app”), published by MullenSoft, collects nothing. Your jams, tunes and recordings stay on your device and, if you use a shared library, in a folder in your own cloud drive. Three features send a value you type or paste to a third party, listed below.

Data collection

Jam Buddy has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Nothing you create is sent to the developer or to any server we operate. We operate no server.

Your library and your cloud drive

If you connect a shared library, Jam Buddy reads and writes files in folders you choose in your own cloud drive (for example iCloud Drive). The developer has no access to those folders. Your use of the cloud drive is governed by its provider's terms.

Third parties that receive what you type or paste

Each of these happens only when you use the feature, and each sends one value that you typed or pasted. The app sends no account, device identifier or device location.

  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: when you look up a jam's location by address, the address text you typed is sent to Nominatim to find its coordinates. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.
  • YouTube: when you paste a YouTube link on a tune, the link is sent to YouTube to fetch a title and performer, and a thumbnail is loaded from YouTube. A pasted YouTube link plays in YouTube's own embedded player, which is YouTube's software and is governed by YouTube's policy. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Slippery Hill: when you paste a Slippery Hill link on a tune, that page is fetched to read its title, with no cookies or credentials. Slippery Hill publishes no separate privacy policy; see slippery-hill.com.

Location

When you tap to use your location, the app reads it on your device, to fill in a jam's coordinates, to tell whether you are at a location-based jam, and to tag a recording's place. It is stored in your own library and never sent to the developer.

Microphone, Photos and Apple Music

Recordings you make are saved on your device and never sent. If you pick a video from Photos, the app reads it on your device to extract its audio, date and location. If you link a song from your Apple Music library, the library is searched on your device. None of this is sent anywhere.

Links to other sites

Links to tune sites such as Slippery Hill, The Session and TuneArch open in your browser. Those sites have their own policies.

This site

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub's standard server logs may record your IP address and the page you requested. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Diagnostics

The app keeps a log on your device to help with support. It leaves your device only if you copy it and send it yourself.

Children's privacy

Jam Buddy collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If we make any material change to this policy, we will post the new version at this URL and update the effective date at the top.
