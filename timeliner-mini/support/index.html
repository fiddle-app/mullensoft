Timeliner Mini — Support

Timeliner Mini is the iPhone companion to Timeliner. Play back a lesson or practice recording, step through its transcript, loop a passage at a slower speed, and keep notes against the exact moment they belong to.

Tips & FAQ

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Timeliner Mini keeps recordings in a shared library: two folders, TuneSync and TuneMedia, that the app creates inside a folder you choose in your own cloud drive. Create one on first launch, or connect to the one your other devices already use. See compatible apps and cloud sync tips.
  • The ＋ button starts a project from a video in Photos, a file in the Files app, or a recording already in your library.
  • The optional transcript runs entirely on your device. If transcription will not start, check Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Speech Recognition ▸ Timeliner Mini.
  • If a recording shows as not downloaded, it is still in your cloud drive; open the project and it downloads.
  • Playback continues with the screen locked, with controls on the lock screen.
