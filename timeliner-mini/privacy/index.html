Timeliner Mini — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Timeliner Mini (“the app”), published by MullenSoft, collects no personal data. Your recordings, transcripts and notes stay on your device and, if you use a shared library, in a folder in your own cloud drive.

Data collection

Timeliner Mini has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Nothing you create is sent to the developer or to any server we operate. We operate no server.

Your library and your cloud drive

Your library is stored on your device. If you create or connect to a shared library, Timeliner Mini reads and writes files in two folders that you choose in your own cloud drive (for example iCloud Drive): one for your data and one for your recordings. That is how your other devices and the other compatible apps see the same library. The developer has no access to those folders. Your use of the cloud drive is governed by its provider's terms.

Transcription (on device)

The optional transcript feature converts speech in a recording to text using Apple's speech recognition with on-device recognition required. Your audio is not sent to Apple's servers, to the developer, or to anyone else. If your device cannot transcribe on-device, the app does not transcribe.

Photos and files

When you start a project from a video in Photos or a file in the Files app, the app copies the one item you pick. It does not ask for access to your photo library and reads nothing else.

Microphone

Timeliner Mini does not record from your microphone. It plays and annotates recordings that already exist.

Network usage

The app sends no information about you over the network. Two help links in the app open pages on mullensoft.com in your browser. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub's standard server logs may record your IP address and the page you requested. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Diagnostics

The app keeps a log on your device to help with support. It leaves your device only if you copy it and send it yourself.

Children's privacy

Timeliner Mini collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If we make any material change to this policy, we will post the new version at this URL and update the effective date at the top.
